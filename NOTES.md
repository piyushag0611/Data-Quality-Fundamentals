## Chapter 1: Why Data Quality Deserves Attention --- Now

- Idea of Data Downtime - when data is simply incorrect. Periods of time when data is missing, inaccurate or erroneous, and it manifests in stale dashboards, inaccurate reports and poor decision making. 

- What is Data Quality? Health of data at any stage in its life cycle. No Data >> Bad Data. 

- Just like DevOps whose goal is to release more reliable and performant software through automation, we have DataOps. DataOps refers to the process of improving the reliability and performance of your data through automation, reducing data silos and fostering quicker, more fault-tolerant analytics.

- Some challenges that have led to the `Rise of Data Downtime`:

    * Migration to the cloud
    * More Data Sources
    * Increasing Complex Data Pipelines
    * More Specialized Data Teams
    * Decentralized Data Teams
